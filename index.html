FAQ

When does my page expire?

At 23:59:59 on the birthday date in the timezone you choose. The server refuses to serve it after that, even if cleanup is delayed.

What happens to my photos?

They are uploaded only when you press Publish and stored on the backend. After expiry they are deleted by a scheduled job with retries. Backups of the hosting provider may keep copies for their own retention period.

Do I need an account?

No. Publishing returns a secret edit token that lets you delete the page early. Keep it private.

Is background music included?

Yes, optional. Four original tracks are generated in the browser (no audio files, no licensing issues) and never start until the visitor taps. You can also add your own licensed audio files. See ASSETS.md.
